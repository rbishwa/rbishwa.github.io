Research program

How physical environments shape cell behavior

My research asks how cells interpret physical and biochemical information from their surroundings and convert those signals into adaptive migratory behavior.

Fluid viscosity and mechanical memory

How extracellular fluid viscosity alters cancer-cell signaling, migration, and long-lasting adaptive states.

Confined cell migration

How cells coordinate polarity, cytoskeletal dynamics, water flux, and edge mechanics while moving through narrow spaces.

Ion transport and cell volume

How channels and transporters organize spatially to control volume, directionality, and adaptation.

Bioelectricity and electrotaxis

How electric fields guide cell migration and interact with membrane potential, calcium, confinement, and mitochondria.

Metabolic adaptation

How physical cues reshape bioenergetics, mitochondrial biology, lipid metabolism, and autophagy.

Active matter and multicellular systems

Connecting principles from active colloids and soft matter to cell motility and cancer–immune interactions.
