Research experience
======
**Johns Hopkins University**, Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering and INBT — 11/2021 – present  
*Graduate Research Assistant* | Advisor: Konstantinos Konstantopoulos, Ph.D.

* Investigated how extracellular viscosity, confinement, matrix stiffness, and electric fields regulate cancer cell migration; demonstrated enhanced 3D invasion through KCC4-dependent signaling at elevated viscosity.
* Formulated tunable polyacrylamide hydrogels and characterized their rheology to relate healthy and tumor-associated breast tissue stiffness to cell behavior.
* Optimized fixation, permeabilization, immunolabeling, and confocal acquisition for quantitative imaging of 2D cultures and 3D samples. Mapped NHE1, KCC4, SWELL1, and cytoskeletal regulators using localization, line-scan, and polarization analyses.
* Engineered optogenetic constructs for targeted protein activation, recruitment, and degradation, with controlled expression and illumination for live-cell imaging.
* Standardized assays across 2D cultures, microfluidic devices, collagen-embedded spheroids, and zebrafish xenografts, including tumor-cell injection at three zebrafish sites. Established quantitative measures of localization, tracking, velocity, direction reversal, and 3D dissemination.
* Designed a microfluidic electrotaxis device to investigate confined cancer cell migration, contractility, and calcium signaling; work presented at conferences and developed into a manuscript in preparation.
* First or co-first author on studies in *Science Advances* (accepted) and *Cell Research*; co-author in *Nature Cell Biology* (accepted), *Oncogene*, and *Cancer Research*.

**Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur**, India — 06/2020 – 05/2021  
*Project Associate* | Advisor: Rahul Mangal, Ph.D.

* Investigated active liquid-crystal droplets and Janus colloids in complex fluids using rheology and optical microscopy; first-authored an Elsevier book chapter.

**Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur**, India — 04/2018 – 06/2019  
*M.S. Research Assistant* | Advisor: Rahul Mangal, Ph.D.

* Studied hydrodynamic and interfacial effects on active Janus colloids and liquid-crystal droplets using rheology, optical microscopy, SEM, and thin-film coating; contributed to papers in *Langmuir* and *Physics of Fluids*.
