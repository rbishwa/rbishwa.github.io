Research experience
======
* **Graduate Research Assistant**, Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore, MD (2021 – Present)
  * Led studies of 4 microenvironmental cues (viscosity, confinement, stiffness, electric fields) in cancer cell migration; showed elevated viscosity enhances 3D invasion via KCC4-dependent signaling.
  * Formulated tunable polyacrylamide hydrogels matching healthy and tumor-associated breast tissue stiffness; characterized gels by rheology to link matrix mechanics to cell behavior.
  * Optimized fixation, permeabilization, immunolabeling, and confocal acquisition for 2D cultures and 3D samples, preserving morphology and signal specificity for quantitative measurement.
  * Mapped 3 ion channels (NHE1, KCC4, SWELL1) and cytoskeletal regulators by immunofluorescence and live-cell imaging, using ROI, line-scan, and polarization analysis.
  * Engineered optogenetic constructs for 3 modes of targeted protein control (activation, recruitment, degradation), tuning expression and illumination for live-cell confocal quantification.
  * Developed 5 quantitative QC metrics (protein localization, cell tracking, migration velocity, direction reversals, 3D dissemination) to benchmark reproducibility across experiments.
  * Standardized workflows across 4 model systems (2D culture, microfluidic migration assays, 3D collagen-embedded spheroids, zebrafish xenografts), with tumor cell injection at 3 different zebrafish sites.
  * Designed a microfluidic electrotaxis device to probe electric-field effects on confined cancer cell migration, contractility, and calcium signaling (3 conference presentations; manuscript in preparation).
  * Generated decision-driving data and contributed to grant writing for one funded NIH R01 and three additional NIH R01 applications, while supporting therapeutic target discovery.
* **Project Associate**, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, India (Jun 2020 – May 2021)
  * Investigated active liquid-crystal droplets and Janus colloids in complex fluids using rheology and optical microscopy; first-authored an Elsevier book chapter.
* **Post-graduate Engineer**, Sterlite Technologies, Pune, India (Jul 2019 – Apr 2020)
  * Completed a three-month training program in optical fiber manufacturing and cabling plants, then identified key process improvements to reduce downtime and enhance operational efficiency of the fiber drawing plant.
* **M.S. Research Assistant**, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, India (2017 – 2019)
  * Investigated active Janus colloids and active liquid-crystal droplets using rheology, optical microscopy, SEM and thin-film coating to study the hydrodynamic and interfacial effects on their motion (2 papers: *Langmuir*, *Physics of Fluids*).
